What this tab does

The Games tab shows where the money is going. This tab adds a second look: how each team has actually played this season. We combine their pace, offense and defense into a rough style total and style margin, then check whether that points the same way as each sharp pick. It is not part of the 1 to 9 grade.

Treat it as an experiment. Teams have played only 1 to 3 games, the numbers are not adjusted for who they played, and nothing here has been tested yet. "Too early" means one of the two teams has played a single game, so we do not lean either way. We will track how it does from Nov 10 on.

How to read the table: pace is possessions per game. Offense and defense are points scored and points allowed per 100 possessions, so a lower defense number is better. eFG% is shooting percentage with threes counted extra. "Avg" is the average of the teams playing today.

How to read thisPlain-English guide to the terms
What is this page?
Each day we compare a computer prediction (KenPom) with what Vegas is offering, then look at where the big money is going. The list on the left shows every game with its grade. Click a game to see everything we found about it. After the games finish we grade every pick so you can see how it did.
KenPom
A college basketball ratings site (kenpom.com) that predicts the winner, the margin and the total points of every game.
Vegas line (DraftKings)
The spread, total and money line the sportsbook offers. We also look at how the line moved since it opened.
Bets and handle
Bets is the share of tickets on a side (how many people). Handle is the share of dollars on a side. Example: 60% of the money but only 40% of the tickets means a few big wagers.
Grade (1 to 9)
A sharp pick's score, 1 plus up to 8 points, built from the two patterns we are tracking. Spread: the side is favored by more than 3 and up to 8 points (4 points), the sharp gap is 15 to 30 (3 points), and the handle is 65 to 80% (1 point). Total: the line moved toward the side (3 points), the sharp gap is 10 to 49 (3 points), and KenPom's total is 2 to 6 points off the line (2 points). The money line has no pattern yet, so it keeps the older strength score. In the list, 7 to 9 is green, 4 to 6 is amber and 1 to 3 is gray, and a game shows its best pick. These patterns were found by looking back at Nov 7 to 9, so the grades fit those games and the real test is games from Nov 10 on. Only sharp picks get a grade.
Style check
A second look, on its own tab, at how each team has played this season: pace, offense, defense, shooting, points in the paint and fast-break points (from CBB Analytics). We turn that into a rough "style total" and "style margin" and see whether it points the same way as each sharp pick on the spread or total. It is an experiment and is not part of the grade. Teams have played only 1 to 3 games, the numbers are not adjusted for opponents, and "Too early" means a team has played just one game.
Sharp
A side where the handle is at least 10 points above the bets. Big money tends to come from experienced bettors.
Public
Everything else: the bets are higher than the handle, or the two are within 10 points.
All three
Three checks pass: KenPom and Vegas point at different sides (or KenPom picked the side), the money is sharp on it, and the line has moved toward that side since it opened.
Original system
KenPom and Vegas disagree, and we look at Vegas's side. For totals it is the side KenPom does not lean toward.
KenPom side
We look at the side KenPom picked (or the Over or Under it leans toward), whether or not Vegas agrees.
Spread, total, money line
Spread: who wins by how many points. Total: Over or Under the combined score. Money line: who wins outright. The money line is only screened when the spread is under 6 points.
Units and results
One unit is risked on every game. Spread and total are graded at -110 (win about 0.91 units, lose 1). Money lines use their own odds.
Small samples
We only have a few days of games, so every record here can change a lot. This page compares data sources and is not betting advice.
Where the numbers come fromSources, timing and limits
Snapshot
KenPom from —, bets and handle from —, lines from —. Lines and splits keep moving, so a game can change by tip-off.
KenPom
Winner, margin, win probability and total come from the KenPom FanMatch page. Early in the season the ratings still lean on preseason priors.
Bets and handle
From VSiN's DraftKings splits.
How teams play
Season-to-date team stats from CBB Analytics (Division I team stats, full season). The Style check tab uses pace, offensive and defensive rating, shooting, three-point rate, paint points and fast-break points.
Lines and movement
Opening and current lines come from DraftKings through ESPN's odds feed. "Moved toward" means the side got stronger since the open: a smaller spread number, shorter odds, or a total that moved toward it. It is shown on every sharp pick but not required.
Money line math
The market probability is the implied probability of the odds as quoted, with the bookmaker's margin left in.
Left out
Games that had already started when the lines were pulled, games without a DraftKings line or a KenPom prediction, and, for the money line only, games with a spread of 6 or more.
Limits
Very small markets can show a huge handle from one or two tickets. Some opening totals look like early placeholders.
